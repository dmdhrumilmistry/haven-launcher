# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

Haven is a home screen (launcher) app. It is built to keep everything on your phone.

## What Haven collects

**Nothing.** Haven has no internet permission, so it cannot send any data anywhere. There are no
ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no accounts.

## What stays on your phone

To work, Haven keeps these on your device only, in its private app storage:

- Your settings: layout, home screen apps and folders, widgets, hidden apps, custom names,
  categories, pause schedules.
- Which apps you open from Haven, with a decaying score, to power "Suggested apps" and "Most used"
  sorting. Turning off Suggested apps erases it.

You can export your settings to a file you choose (Settings > Backup). Uninstalling Haven deletes
everything it stored. Android's own backup may include Haven's settings if you have device backup
turned on; that is controlled by your phone, not by Haven.

## Optional permissions

These are off until you turn them on, and only exist in the "full" version:

- **Accessibility service** (double-tap to lock): only used to lock the screen and open the
  notification shade when you ask with a gesture. It does not read what's on your screen.
- **Notification access** (icon badges): only used to count how many notifications each app has.
  Haven never reads, stores or shares notification content.

Other permissions: expanding the status bar (swipe down for notifications), opening the system
uninstall dialog, fingerprint/face prompt (for locked hidden apps), and seeing Android 15's private
space. None of them involve your personal data leaving the device.
