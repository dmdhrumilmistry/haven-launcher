Haven is a calm, clean home screen that feels like stock Android, with the features you'd expect from a modern launcher, and none of the noise. No ads, no tracking, no accounts, and no internet permission, so nothing ever leaves your phone.

<b>Home screen</b>
• Clock, date and your next alarm
• Icon grid (3 to 6 columns), names only, or icons with names
• Long-press and drag to rearrange; drop an app onto another to make a folder
• Widgets: add, resize, reorder and remove
• Wallpaper-aware text color, optional dim
• Pinned shortcuts from other apps ("Add to home screen")

<b>App drawer</b>
• Swipe up, search instantly: ignores accents, matches initials ("gm" finds Google Maps), Enter opens the top result
• Built-in calculator in search
• Grid or list, A-Z scroller in every language, or sort by most used
• Suggested apps from your own habits, learned only on your phone
• Your own categories as tabs, plus folders by drag and drop
• A dot on new apps you haven't opened yet

<b>Work profile, private space and clones</b>
• Swipe between Personal, Work and Private tabs
• Pause and resume work apps, lock and unlock Android 15's private space
• Cloned apps show with a "Clone" tag; "Clone app" opens your phone's cloning screen where it has one

<b>Privacy and focus</b>
• Hide apps, and lock them with your fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Pause distracting apps for a while or on a schedule
• Shortcut to Digital Wellbeing's app timer for hard limits

<b>Make it yours</b>
• Light, dark or system theme; Material You colors
• Icon packs, icon shapes, themed (monochrome) icons
• Notification badges as a dot or a number
• Double-tap to lock, swipe down for notifications
• Rename apps; export and import all your settings

<b>Permissions, plainly</b>
Haven has no internet access. Double-tap to lock uses an accessibility service only to lock the screen; notification badges only count notifications and never read them. Both are off until you turn them on.

Haven is free and open source (GPL-3.0).
